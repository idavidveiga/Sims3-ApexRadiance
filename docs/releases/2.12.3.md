# Apex Radiance 2.12.3

2026-10-13 · [v2.12.3](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.12.3) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Outdoor lamp light that follows distance and buildings, and faster lamp edits

## New features

- **Realistic lamp reach:** lamp light on outside walls fades with distance as it does on the ground, instead of staying at full brightness for about 9 m and reaching far upper stories. On by default, in Lighting > Buildings. Outdoor objects, fences and windows then take the same light as the walls beside them.

## Changes

- **Buildings block light on objects** (was *Walls block light on objects*): the floors of balconies and upper stories also block a lamp below them, for windows, objects and fences as well as walls and floors. A lamp seen past a balcony's edge is dimmed rather than cut.
- **Light behind balconies** is removed: the wall behind a balcony no longer takes light from a lamp under the balcony floor. Saved settings and presets that still hold it load as before.
- **Lamp edits all at once** (was *Lamp switches all at once*): after any lamp edit (a switch, a colour or intensity change, a move, a new or deleted lamp) the lamp's own floor shows its new light as soon as it is ready, and the other floors change together. Edited rooms are relit sooner. While a lamp edit is pending, its rooms are also relit on neighbouring lots and while the camera moves (up to 6 ms per frame).

## Fixes

- A lamp under a balcony no longer lights the walls, windows, objects and fences above the balcony floor.
- Tops of fences, railings and objects standing above every nearby lamp no longer take ground light.
- Outdoor lamps no longer light walls, objects and ground in daylight. A lamp left on by day now lights walls, ground, lot floors, roads, roofs, water, most outdoor objects, fences, windows and the rooms it shines into with one daytime curve: 3% of its night strength in full daylight, rising through dusk to its unchanged night strength. Indoor lamps are not affected.

## Known limitations

- A balcony floor blocks only light going up: a lamp on a balcony still lights the wall below it, and split-level floors may be off.
- The wall behind a balcony takes no light from a lamp below it; light bouncing off the balcony or the ground is not modelled.
- Rooms lit through their windows by an outdoor lamp update once the day or the night has settled, not continuously through dusk.
- Foliage, grass and a few outdoor objects the mod does not redraw still take a lamp's full light by day, and lamp light on the ground and on foliage is not yet blocked by walls.
- In-game confirmation of these changes on the release build is pending; offline checks and both builds pass.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game and launcher, then replace **ApexRadiance.asi** in **The Sims 3\Game\Bin\**. Existing settings and presets remain compatible; the removed *Light behind balconies* values are ignored. No additional DLL is required.
