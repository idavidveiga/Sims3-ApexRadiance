# Apex Radiance 2.7.2

Published 2026-10-06 · tag [`v2.7.2`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.7.2) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Windows take the outdoor light, and water keeps its reflections in rain and snow.

## New

- **Windows take outdoor light** (Lighting > Objects, on): the outer side of windows and doors lit by the room takes the
  outdoor ground light and lamps instead of staying black, and a window takes the same lamps whichever way it is turned
  ([objects-and-rigs.md](../features/night-lighting/objects-and-rigs.md)).

## Improvements

- **Lakes in rain and snow:** lamp glow and shore reflections, as on dry days. The game draws these lakes with another
  shader pair that Apex did not know, so they stayed dark at night ([water.md](../features/night-lighting/water.md)).
- **Twinbrook's sea in rain:** the shore reflection pass, as in dry weather.

## Fixes

- **Fences and curbs:** with per-pixel lamps, faces turned up keep the ground light (a curb's top went nearly black at
  night where no lamp reached).
- **Small worlds:** the end of the scene needs 8 depth-tested draws instead of 20; a world in Create a World drew 13 at
  some angles, and Color and the screen effects came and went with the camera.
## Changes

- New shader identities: `kLakeRainPs`, `kLakeSnowPs`, `kLakeWeatherVs` (pass compiled with `WEATHER`) and
  `kSeaNoReflRainPs` (the sea pass).
- `LevelLightShare::OnWallLine` and `ObjectLampPoint` for the window lamp point.
