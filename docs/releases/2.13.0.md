# Apex Radiance 2.13.0

2026-10-14 · [v2.13.0](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.13.0) · ApexRadiance.asi

## Highlights

Instant lamp lighting on the graphics card, and lamp light through doors, windows and arches

## New features

- **Lamp lighting:** a new card at the top of System > Performance chooses how lot lamps update after you switch,
  recolour or move one. *Instant (GPU)*, the default, has the graphics card draw the active lot's outdoor lamps with
  real shadows, so every floor updates at once; moving lamps may lower the frame rate a little. *Classic* is the
  lighter option: the game recalculates the rooms' light, a dragged lamp's light updates once it is released, and all
  floors change together. Indoor lamps use the game's calculation in both modes.
- **Outdoor light through openings:** garden lamps light porches, arcades and rooms with doors or windows through
  those openings, including lamps on the story below that see an arch. On by default, in Lighting > Buildings > Rooms
  at Night.
- **Indoor light through openings:** lit rooms shine through their arches and windows onto the garden, terraces,
  outside walls and objects outside, and into a roofed room next door.
- **Doors block light:** closed doors and gates stop lamp light; arches, windows and doors with glass let it through.
- **Walls shade lamp light near the ground** (Lighting > Ground): grass, paths, decks, curbs, stairs and low fences
  behind an outside wall stay dark instead of taking a garden lamp's light through the wall.
- **Shadow softness:** light past an opening, a wall's end or top and a balcony's edge keeps a sharp edge next to it
  and softens farther away, by the lamp's size (15 cm by default, 2 to 50 cm).
- **Smooth light maps:** lamp light in rooms is drawn without blocks, bands or rings.

## Changes

- **Buildings block lamp light on trees and objects** (Lighting > Objects; was *Buildings block light on
  objects*): bushes and trees behind a wall or a house no longer take a garden lamp's light through it.
- **Smoother lamp dragging:** while a lamp is dragged, the extra room updates wait until it stops, and the rooms of
  the edit are updated in smaller steps afterwards.
- **Rooms at Night** groups its options under *Background light* and *Lamp light*; the openings, doors, shadow
  softness and smooth light map options are there.
- Less work per frame for drawing and lighting: fewer per-draw lookups and checks in the graphics hooks.

## Fixes

- Lamps a player disabled no longer light nearby objects.
- Outdoor lamps no longer light foliage by day.
- Garden lamps reach colonnades and porches on a foundation.
- Columns inside arches take the lamp light that reaches them through the arch.
- Light under houses on foundations is no longer stopped by a false ceiling.
- Railings and fences on upper stories no longer pick up light from ground lamps.

## Known limitations

- *Instant (GPU)* works on The Sims 3 Steam 1.67.2 and needs Night Lights; on another game version or a graphics card
  that cannot run it, the card says why and lamps update as in *Classic*.
- *Instant (GPU)* covers the active lot's outdoor lamps. Other lots, world grass, roads and street lamps keep the game's
  light; rooms lit through openings and their objects follow at the game's pace.
- Objects, Sims and terrain do not cast lamp shadows.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game and launcher, then replace **ApexRadiance.asi** in **The Sims 3\Game\Bin\**. Existing settings and
presets remain compatible; Lamp lighting starts on *Instant (GPU)* and a saved choice is kept. No additional DLL is
required.
