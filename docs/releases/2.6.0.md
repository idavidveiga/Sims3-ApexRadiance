# Apex Radiance 2.6.0

Published 2026-10-05 · tag [`v2.6.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.6.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Sim Occlusion, a welcome screen and a cleaner menu.

## New features

- **Sim Occlusion:** a new card under Image that softens Ambient Occlusion on Sims without weakening it on the rest of the scene. Skin and clothing, and hair, have separate strengths, and Maximum darkening caps how dark a Sim can get. It is off by default and needs Ambient Occlusion on.
- **Transparent hair and coverage preview:** semi-transparent hair strands follow the hair strength, and Show Sim coverage paints the pixels each control affects, so you can see exactly what changes.
- **Ambient Occlusion distance:** a new Distance slider sets how far away the shade fades out.
- **Welcome screen:** new installations open the menu on a short welcome with three ready-made profiles, Performance, Default and Quality. Clicking one applies it right away, so you can compare them in the game before you continue, or keep your own settings. Existing players are not shown it.
- **Built-in profiles:** the same three profiles are always available in Settings > Profiles, above your own, and the profile icon picker has 30 new icons.
- **Filtered screenshots on F8:** press F8 to save one screenshot with every Apex effect applied. The game's own C screenshot is no longer replaced.
- **Choose the folder:** save to the game's Screenshots folder or to a Screenshots folder inside Apex Radiance.
- **Hide the game UI:** the game's interface can be hidden for the photo and restored right after.
- **Attention page:** appears only when something outside Apex blocks an effect, such as the game's own Edge Smoothing or a room color saved in Sims3SettingsSetter, and explains how to fix it. When nothing needs you, it is not in the menu.
- **What's new:** click the version at the bottom of the menu to see the changes in each release.

## Changes

- **Hide the menu with Alt:** holding Alt over the menu now hides it completely instead of making it see-through.
- **Cleaner header and footer:** the header is a single line with every control at the same height, and the footer shows the save status, the version in the middle and the Alt hint. A button in the footer switches the sidebar between icons with names and icons only.
- **Consistent controls:** buttons, fields and rows share the same sizes and alignment on every page, and the Lighting page groups its options more clearly, with fine tuning under Advanced.
- **Editable shortcuts:** in Settings > Shortcuts, pick a set (letters, numbers or F keys) and change any shortcut you like.
- **Clearer profiles:** applying a profile shows a selection card where you choose which kinds of settings it changes.
- **Refresh lighting for everyone:** Lighting > Overview has a Refresh lighting button that relights rooms, lots and objects, without Developer mode.
- **Simpler controls:** the reset buttons on each page and card were removed. Every control still has its own default, and Settings keeps the option to reset everything.
- **Optimize rendering removed:** the switch is gone and Apex always renders the same way it did before 2.5.6.
- **Indoor light across stories:** indoor lamps light the stories above and below through stairwells, atriums and removed floors, across more than one story, while closed floors and walls still block them.
- **Faster updates after building:** adding a story, a roof or closing a room updates the indoor light much sooner.
- **More features on the EA app version:** Faster Memory, Faster Room Lighting, Rooms keep their light, seamless walls between floors and the indoor light between stories now work on the EA app version of the game, as on Steam.

## Fixes

- **Ground brightness everywhere:** Ground brightness now applies to every kind of terrain, so changing it no longer leaves a visible edge at lot borders, in grass or in snow.
- **No light through walls:** lamp light no longer leaks onto the ground outside a closed wall, or onto furniture in raised rooms on other stories.
- **Daytime terrain:** lamp light on the ground blends the same way with daylight on every kind of terrain, so lot edges no longer show a brighter or darker strip during the day.
- **Rooms at Night with Sims3SettingsSetter:** Rooms at Night now works when Sims3SettingsSetter has a saved room color. Removing that saved color is optional and only happens when you choose it on the Attention page, after a backup.
- **Effects with the game UI hidden:** Ambient Occlusion, Edge Smoothing and Depth Blur keep working when you hide the game's interface.
- **A clean end of loading:** Depth Blur and the start message wait until the world has finished loading, and Depth Blur fades in instead of blurring the whole screen for a moment.
- **Cheats are safe to type:** typing in the game's cheat console no longer triggers Apex shortcuts.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
