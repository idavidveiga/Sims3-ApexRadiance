# Apex Radiance 2.7.0

Published 2026-10-06 · tag [`v2.7.0`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.7.0) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Filters, lamp switches in one frame and lot streaming.

## New features

- **Filters:** a new Filters tab in Image > Color with 26 looks you can stack, each with its own strength: Technicolor 1 and 2, DPX Cineon, Vintage, Cross-process, Filmic pass, Black and white, Tint, Colorfulness, Night Mode, Levels, LUT, Atmospheric fog, Auto exposure, Adaptive sharpening, Glow, Halation, Dreamy, Fake HDR, Emphasize, Tilt-shift, Prism, Film grain, 3DFX, CRT and Color-blind mode. All start off, and the game's interface is never filtered.
- **LUT files:** put PNG look-up tables (Lightroom, Photoshop or ReShade LUT packs) in the LUTs folder of Apex Radiance and pick one in the LUT filter.
- **Lamp switches all at once (Experimental):** switching one lamp or all the lights of a house now changes the rooms, the furniture, the ground and the trees together, in a single frame, once the new light is ready. No steps and no corrections afterwards. On by default under Performance > Faster room lighting.
- **Lot Streaming (Experimental):** lots keep their full detail farther away (up to 300 m) and more of them at once (up to 16), streaming in smoothly while the camera moves, with steadier lot visibility and a pause in map view. Off by default: turn it on in its own page under System. Research and code by idavidveiga.
- **Ambient Occlusion:** new Temporal smoothing, Half resolution and Thin object detail options, less grain with a long reach and no grain on rails and posts.
- **Light detail:** a new Lighting option for sharper lamp light on walls and floors.
- **Room to save (Experimental):** keeps memory free for saving (Error 12) and drops unused game files when memory runs low, also on the EA app version.
- **Lighter window updates (Experimental):** the game stops repainting its own window every frame.
- **Faster scripts (Experimental):** the game's scripts compare numbers and look up types with less work.

## Changes

- **Spread lot lighting while moving:** lots that stream in while the camera moves now build in small steps too, for fewer hitches.
- **Spread new objects over frames:** a busy moment no longer waits for every queued object at once, for fewer hitches when a lot streams in.
- **Faster object lookups:** the game finds objects by their ID from an index, for fewer hitches when lot lights update.
- **Faster game file lookups:** fewer repeated checks of the game's packages while files load.
- **No long frame at load:** rebuilding the ground light when the world loads or with Refresh lighting runs one piece at a time instead of freezing the picture for a moment.
- **Lighter on memory:** Apex keeps fewer shader objects and no longer loads a shader compiler into the game.
- **Less work while drawing:** Apex asks the graphics card for less while it draws, and rooms with no light skip work when objects are removed.
- **Ground in one frame:** the ground around switched lamps changes all at once, without quarters or seams between pieces of terrain.
- **Furniture across stories:** indoor furniture takes the light of lamps on the story above or below that reach it through openings.
- **Faster light after loading:** a lot you enter corrects its lighting in one round, sooner.
- **Lamp edits in Build mode:** moving or switching a lamp updates its rooms sooner and keeps the frame rate steady.
- **Light styles:** the walls take a better balanced brightness in each style, and Soft is the default.
- **Banding Fix page:** Banding Fix has its own page, and Smooth gradients is off by default.
- **Menu from the world selector:** the menu and the start note are available from the world selector on.
- **Built-in profiles:** they leave Water & Snow, Banding and Lot Streaming as you set them. Sim Occlusion is on by default.
- **Report a problem:** recordings also measure how the light updates on screen after a lamp switch.

## Fixes

- **Walls block lamp light outdoors:** lamp light no longer passes through walls onto outdoor floors, objects and fences, and fences take lamp light per pixel.
- **Upper half walls:** a wall lamp of the ground floor now also lights the half wall of a balcony above it, as it lights the wall beside it.
- **Lot edges in any weather:** lot borders stay seamless in rain, snow and melting snow.
- **Water reflections in Twinbrook, Bridgeport and Moonlight Falls:** the water of these worlds that never showed the game's reflection now gets the shore reflection.
- **Effects wait for the world:** Color, Ambient Occlusion, Edge Smoothing and Depth Blur stay off on the main menu and loading screens until the world is drawn.
- **Pie menu:** the pie menu and its Sim portrait no longer leave an unfiltered box or a grey square with the Color filters and fog.
- **Depth Blur:** no blur over the frozen picture of the save screen.
- **AMD integrated graphics:** on PCs with a dedicated graphics card and an AMD integrated GPU, the unused AMD Vulkan driver now really stays out of the game, leaving it more memory.

## Known limitations

Lamp switches all at once and the new lighting changes were tested by the author in game on the Steam 1.67.2 version
only. Lot Streaming was researched on the EA app version (1.69) and ported to Steam; its Steam addresses were found
offline, in the game file.

## Upgrade notes

Close the game and launcher, back up the previous ASI, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Keep only one Apex Radiance ASI in that folder. Existing settings are retained.
