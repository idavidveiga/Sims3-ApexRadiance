# Apex Radiance 2.8.1

Published 2026-10-07 · tag [`v2.8.1`](https://github.com/loinyx/Sims3-ApexRadiance/releases/tag/v2.8.1) · asset `ApexRadiance.asi`.

## Highlights

Doors keep the light of their own side, snowy ground under rugs matches the lot, and the Banding Fix defaults are the same in every profile.

## Improvements

- **Banding Fix defaults:** on, Strength 100%, Moving grain off and Smooth gradients 8%, as the default and in every built-in
  profile ([banding-fix.md](../features/banding-fix.md)).

## Fixes

- **Doors and windows set in a wall:** with the indoor smooth light, they took up to ~40% of the room behind the wall (a
  sconce's colour on the dark side's door frame); they keep the game's light of the camera's room, as before Apex.
- **Snow under rugs:** the game draws the ground under a rug with a footprint-cut variant of the snowy lot light pass, which
  Apex did not know: it missed the lot's terrain light and showed as a darker square.

## Changes

- New shader identities `kSnowLotCutPs` / `kSnowLotCutVs`; `DrawLotSnow` takes the cut variant (terrain light in s13).
- `SetInWall` in `DrawIndoorObject` (outside wall line, or the object's tile centre on a room-map tile edge).
