## Clearer lighting controls and simpler captures

- **Night Lighting:** Choose Discreet, Soft or Natural lighting from three clear options. Your current balance stays in place until you choose a style, and Undo restores your previous adjustments.
- **Simpler problem reports:** Restored the familiar capture layout. After saving, you can add an optional title and description; blank fields keep automatic diagnostic details. Existing captures are preserved.
- **One build:** Developer tools are now available through an optional mode in Settings, with confirmation and a restart. Normal play keeps developer mode off by default.
- **Edge Smoothing:** FXAA is listed first and recommended. SMAA remains available without temporal smoothing.
- **Screen controls:** Removed Apex's window, V-Sync and FPS controls. Configure those through the game, driver or another controller. G-SYNC and FreeSync remain externally configured.
- **Existing settings:** Older profiles keep their lighting and visual settings; retired screen-control entries are ignored. Updated instructions are available in English, Portuguese, Spanish and French.

## Install

Close the game, then replace `ApexRadiance.asi` in `The Sims 3\Game\Bin\`. Your settings are kept.
